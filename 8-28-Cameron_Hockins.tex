\section{Iterative-Deepening DFS (IDDFS)}

\textbf{Idea:} DFS is light on memory, but can overshoot the goal --- limiting the depth and gradually increasing it fixes this.

\begin{algorithm}[h]
\caption{Iterative-Deepening Search}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{Iterative-Deepening-Search}{problem}
    \For{depth $= 0$ to $\infty$}
        \State result $\leftarrow$ \Call{Depth-Limited-Search}{problem, depth}
        \If{result $\neq$ cutoff}
            \State \Return result
        \EndIf
    \EndFor
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsection{Best-First Search / Uniform Cost Search}

This considers the cost to explore a path, and chooses the lowest-cost path first.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Property} & \textbf{Result} \\
\midrule
Complete & Yes, if all step costs are above $\epsilon$ \\
 & (costs keep decreasing for each step: $1, \tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{1}{4}, \dots$) \\
Optimal & Yes \\
Time & $O\!\left(b^{1 + \lfloor c/\epsilon \rfloor}\right)$ \\
Space & $O\!\left(b^{1 + \lfloor c/\epsilon \rfloor}\right)$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Properties of Uniform Cost Search}
\end{table}
